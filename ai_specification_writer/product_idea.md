# Product Idea: CarpoolConnect

## Vision
CarpoolConnect is a corporate commute management platform that helps employees share rides to and from work safely, conveniently, and affordably. Its mission is to reduce commuting costs, parking demand, traffic congestion, and transportation-related emissions while strengthening connections among coworkers.

## Primary Users and Needs
- **Employee Drivers:** Want to offer available seats on their commute, coordinate pickups easily, and share commuting expenses.
- **Employee Passengers:** Want reliable, affordable rides with verified coworkers and clear pickup and drop-off details.
- **HR and Workplace Administrators:** Want to encourage sustainable commuting, manage employee access, and understand participation and commuting trends.
- **Facilities and Sustainability Teams:** Want aggregate insights into parking demand, shared rides, and estimated environmental impact.

## Key Features
1. **Corporate Account Verification:** Employees join an approved organization using company-managed invitations or verified work email addresses.
2. **Ride Offers and Requests:** Drivers publish routes, schedules, meeting points, and available seats; passengers search and request rides.
3. **Commute Matching:** Match employees based on route proximity, work schedules, available seats, and preferences.
4. **Booking and Trip Coordination:** Confirm seats, notify participants of updates, and provide pickup instructions.
5. **Recurring Commutes:** Allow users to schedule regular journeys on workdays without recreating each ride.
6. **Ratings and Safety Tools:** Support post-trip feedback, reporting of inappropriate behavior, and organization-level moderation.
7. **Cost Sharing:** Provide transparent, configurable suggested contributions; actual payment processing may be added in a later phase.
8. **Impact Dashboard:** Show aggregated ride-sharing adoption, estimated emissions avoided, and parking-demand trends.
9. **Privacy Controls:** Share precise pickup details only with confirmed trip participants and minimize employee location data retention.

## Differentiators
- **Workplace-focused trust:** Organization-verified membership instead of a fully open public carpool marketplace.
- **Routine-first matching:** Optimized for repeated home-to-work travel and shift-based schedules.
- **Employer insights with employee privacy:** Aggregate sustainability and utilization metrics without exposing individual commute histories to administrators.
- **Practical adoption features:** Recurring rides, clear coordination, and configurable company commuting policies.

## Initial MVP Scope
The first release will include verified employee accounts, ride creation and search, seat requests and confirmations, basic notifications, recurring commute preferences, and an administrative summary dashboard. Payment processing, live GPS tracking, and advanced route optimization are explicitly deferred until later releases.

## Success Criteria
- Employees can create, find, request, and confirm a shared commute.
- Organization administrators can manage employee access and view aggregate participation metrics.
- Confirmed riders receive essential trip details, while personal commute information remains appropriately restricted.
